# Day 51 – Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## 📌 What I Worked On

Today was dedicated to planning the capstone project rather than implementation. I focused on identifying a realistic product idea, defining the MVP, and preparing a structured roadmap for the next ten days.

## 🎯 Key Learnings

* Product discovery is as important as coding.
* A well-defined MVP helps avoid unnecessary complexity.
* Clear requirements reduce redesign during development.
* Daily milestones make large projects manageable.
* Planning first leads to faster implementation later.
